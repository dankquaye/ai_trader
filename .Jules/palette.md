## 2024-05-22 - Modal Overlay Blocking Due to Missing Tailwind Utilities\n**Learning:** Missing utility classes (`.opacity-0`, `.pointer-events-none`) in Tailwind setups without a build step can silently break overlay interactions, causing the app to feel frozen.\n**Action:** Always verify critical utility classes are available in `index.html`'s `<style>` block if a full build pipeline is absent.
